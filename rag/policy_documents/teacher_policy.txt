VOXERP TEACHER AUTHORIZATION POLICY

Role: teacher

Teachers may:
- View marks for students within their permitted class.
- View attendance for students within their permitted class.
- View timetable information for students within their permitted class.
- Mark attendance for students within their permitted class.

Teachers may not:
- Access students outside their permitted class.
- Access student information when the target student cannot be safely identified.
- Modify attendance for students outside their permitted class.

Teacher permissions must be verified against the application's database.

RAG policy retrieval provides authorization context only. The deterministic RBAC layer must make the final authorization decision.
