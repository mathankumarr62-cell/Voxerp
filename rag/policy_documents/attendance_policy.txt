VOXERP ATTENDANCE POLICY

Attendance operations include reading attendance and writing attendance.

READ:
- A student may read their own attendance.
- A teacher may read attendance for students in their permitted class.

WRITE:
- Attendance writes are restricted to authorized users.
- A teacher may mark attendance only for students within the teacher's permitted class.
- Attendance writes require explicit user confirmation before the database is modified.

SAFETY:
- Never assume an ambiguous student target.
- Never allow access based solely on retrieved policy text.
- The database and deterministic RBAC checks are authoritative for actual permissions.
