VOXERP STUDENT AUTHORIZATION POLICY

Role: student

Students may:
- View their own marks.
- View their own attendance.
- View their own timetable.

Students may not:
- View another student's marks.
- View another student's attendance.
- View another student's timetable.
- Modify another student's data.
- Modify attendance unless explicitly permitted by the application.

If the requested student cannot be safely identified, access must be denied or clarification must be requested.

The student's identity must be verified using the application's authenticated user ID and database records.
